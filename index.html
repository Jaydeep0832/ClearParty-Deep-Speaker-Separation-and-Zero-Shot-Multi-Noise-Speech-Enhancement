Engineering Progression

Evolution of the Project: Four Developmental Milestones

From classical STFT magnitude masking limitations to scaled time-domain Conv-TasNet, natural Wiener filtering, and zero-shot multi-noise SOTA enhancement.

MILESTONE 1 (BASELINE)

Phase 1: Spectrogram U-Net & The "Phase Catastrophe"

The Hypothesis: Apply 2D Convolutional U-Net on Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) magnitude spectrograms to predict magnitude ratio masks, inverting via inverse STFT (iSTFT).

The Bottleneck: Reconstructing separated audio using the noisy mixture's phase creates a severe mathematical ceiling (< +6.0 dB SI-SDRi). Phase mismatch causes audible comb-filtering and watery synthetic artifacts. Classical Independent Component Analysis (ICA) similarly failed because single-microphone mixtures break ICA's spatial independence assumptions.

MILESTONE 2 (BREAKTHROUGH)

Phase 2A: Time-Domain 1D Conv-TasNet Baseline

The Breakthrough: Abandoned STFT time-frequency representations entirely. Adopted 1D Time-Domain Audio Separation Network (Conv-TasNet) operating directly on raw discrete audio waveforms.

Trained on 2,700 Libri2Mix pairs with 1.4M parameters using Utterance-level Permutation Invariant Training (uPIT). Completely bypassed the phase estimation bottleneck, achieving +6.98 dB test SI-SDRi.

MILESTONE 3 (SCALING & OPTIMIZATION)

Phase 2B: Scaled 14k Dataset & Mixed-Precision Hardware Acceleration

The Optimization: Scaled from 2,700 pairs to 14,269 mixture pairs (251 speakers) and doubled network capacity to 3.4M parameters (3,396,144 params).

  • • GPU Memory & Precision: Migrated to FP16 / Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP), halving memory consumption.
  • • Throughput: Increased batch size to 12 and staged data to local NVMe SSD, slashing epoch time from 14.0 min to 4.5 min on NVIDIA Tesla T4 (3.1× speedup).
  • • Benchmark Leap: Validation SI-SDR surged to +11.76 dB; Mean Test SI-SDRi leaped to +11.06 dB (+4.08 dB generalization boost).
MILESTONE 4 (POST-FILTERING & POLISH)

Phase 3: Held-Out 200 Test Benchmark & Spectral Wiener Post-Filter

The Innovation: Rigorous evaluation on 200 held-out test mixtures revealed that raw time-domain masks occasionally produced a faint metallic timbre.

Engineered a specialized Spectral Wiener Post-Filtering Module ($W_k(f,t)$) that re-synthesizes organic vocal tract overtones. Achieved +13.13 dB Peak SI-SDRi, raised PESQ from 1.59 to 2.30 (+0.71 delta), and elevated STOI intelligibility from 71.3% to 88.1%.

MILESTONE 5 (SOTA ENHANCEMENT)

Phase 4: Zero-Shot Multi-Noise Generalization & SOTA ClearVoice FRCRN

The Generalization Challenge: Can networks trained strictly on single noises generalize zero-shot to complex overlapping compound environments (Babble + Street + Airport)?

Benchmarked 1D Conv-TasNet against ClearVoice FRCRN-16K (Complex STFT CFSMN with sinc-resampling). On a continuous 6.79s LibriSpeech stress utterance, FRCRN achieved +7.22 dB SI-SDR gain (+4.99 → +12.21 dB), 2.542 PESQ (+1.112 gain), 80.89% direct noise energy attenuation, and 97.12% clean waveform correlation ($r=0.9712$).

Scale-Invariant SDR (SI-SDR)

Standard SNR penalizes arbitrary scale mismatches. SI-SDR orthogonally projects estimated signal onto target:

\alpha = \frac{\hat{s}^T s}{\|s\|^2 + \epsilon}, \quad e_{tgt} = \alpha s, \quad e_{noise} = \hat{s} - e_{tgt}
\text{SI-SDR} = 10 \log_{10} \left( \frac{\|e_{tgt}\|^2}{\|e_{noise}\|^2 + \epsilon} \right)

Utterance-level PIT (uPIT)

Resolves permutation ambiguity (which output corresponds to which speaker) by finding lowest permutation loss:

\mathcal{L}_{uPIT} = \min_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \sum_{i=1}^2 -\text{SI-SDR}(s_i, \hat{s}_{p(i)})

Computes all 2! = 2 pairings: $(s_1, \hat{s}_1) + (s_2, \hat{s}_2)$ vs $(s_1, \hat{s}_2) + (s_2, \hat{s}_1)$, backpropagating only on the optimal assignment.

Natural Spectral Wiener Filter

Post-processing module smoothing metallic artifacts by re-synthesizing natural vocal tract resonances:

W_k(f, t) = \frac{|\hat{S}_k(f, t)|^2}{|\hat{S}_1(f, t)|^2 + |\hat{S}_2(f, t)|^2 + \epsilon}
S_k^{Wiener}(f, t) = W_k(f, t) \cdot X_{mix}(f, t)

★ Why choose time-domain Conv-TasNet over traditional STFT spectrogram masking? ▼
STFT magnitude masking creates the "Phase Catastrophe": recovering clean magnitudes while recombining with the noisy mixture phase introduces comb-filtering and bounds SI-SDRi below +6.0 dB. Conv-TasNet operates directly on discrete time-domain samples via a 1D convolutional encoder and decoder, optimizing both magnitude and phase implicitly.
★ What is Permutation Invariant Training (uPIT) and why is it mandatory? ▼
In blind speaker separation, the order of target speakers ($s_1, s_2$) is arbitrary. If the network outputs $\hat{s}_1, \hat{s}_2$, assigning static targets causes gradient cancellation because the model gets penalized for assigning speaker A to channel 2 even if the separation was perfect. uPIT evaluates all $N!$ permutations and backpropagates gradients only through the minimum-loss permutation.
★ What is the purpose of the Spectral Wiener Post-Filter ($W_k(f,t)$)? ▼
Raw time-domain continuous masks occasionally introduce high-frequency synthetic metallic artifacts. The spectral Wiener filter computes a soft ratio mask $W_k(f,t) = |\hat{S}_k|^2 / (|\hat{S}_1|^2 + |\hat{S}_2|^2 + \epsilon)$ in the STFT domain, re-weighting the original mixture to preserve organic vocal tract overtones and produce natural human voice restoration.
Why use Scale-Invariant SDR (SI-SDR) instead of Mean Squared Error (MSE)? ▼
Waveform MSE is hypersensitive to global gain differences. A separated signal that is perfectly isolated but scaled by a factor of 0.8 would yield a large MSE penalty. SI-SDR calculates an orthogonal projection $\alpha = \hat{s}^T s / \|s\|^2$, decomposing the estimate into an aligned target vector and an orthogonal residual error, measuring pure separation fidelity.
Why use Dilated Depthwise Separable Convolutions instead of LSTMs/BLSTMs? ▼
Recurrent networks like BLSTM have high computational complexity, cannot process long sequences in parallel during training, and suffer from vanishing gradients over long audio files. Exponentially dilated depthwise separable 1D convolutions expand the receptive field to 153 ms while reducing parameter count by over 80% and allowing complete parallelization across GPU tensor cores.
